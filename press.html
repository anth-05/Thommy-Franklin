Short bio

Thommy Franklin is a DJ and producer born and raised in Amsterdam. He grew up in a house full of music and played piano and guitar as a kid, but sport always came first, until he couldn't play anymore and music took its place. He started DJing in August 2022 and producing in November 2023. His sets run from 127 BPM house to 140 BPM speed garage, built on strong, punchy rhythms and catchy vocals that keep coming back and changing over time. Inspired by Interplanetary Criminal, Overheat, MUCKANIKS and the Swingers label, he wants to bring the dancefloor back to what it was made for: forgetting about Monday for a couple of hours and dancing with people who love the same music.
