Short bio

Thommy Franklin is a UK Garage & House producer and DJ from the Netherlands. After a national-level sports career in football and basketball ended with an ankle injury, music became his new identity. He blends nostalgic samples with modern sound, drawing on years of playing guitar, drums and piano, and brings that energy to every set. His mission: bring people together through music that makes everyone want to move at once.
