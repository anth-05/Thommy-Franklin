# Product

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static multi-page HTML (`index.html` with hero, music, story, upcoming events and Wildcard; `press.html` and `booking.html` as their own pages) sharing `assets/site.css` and `assets/site.js`. Node/Express backend in `server/` serves the pages and an `/api` (booking enquiries by email via SMTP; new features mount as routers). See README.md. Previewed as a Claude artifact link; will be hosted on Thommy's own domain later, where embedded players, a sending form and file downloads become possible.

## Users

Primary: promoters, bookers, labels and press who received a link from Thommy's outreach (Months 5–7 of his artist plan). They check within seconds whether he is worth booking or signing: his sound, story, proof of live play, and how to reach him.

Secondary: fans arriving from TikTok / Instagram Reels / Stories, mostly on phones, who want to listen and follow.

## Product Purpose

The site is Thommy Franklin's portfolio and EPK in one link. It replaces a static PDF press kit, lets bookers hear his music and read his story fast, and turns interest into a booking or press enquiry. Success: enquiries and EPK requests from organizers and labels.

## Positioning

An Amsterdam DJ and producer (DJ since Aug 2022, producing since Nov 2023) playing 127 BPM house to 140 BPM speed garage. His sound is rhythm-heavy: strong, punchy grooves made for dancing, with a catchy vocal that returns and changes, and only occasional melody. Grew up with music at home, played piano and guitar as a kid; sport came first until he couldn't play anymore and music took its place. Mission in his words: two hours of forgetting about Monday and dancing with people who love the same music, bringing the dancefloor back to dancing.

Source of truth for site copy: Thommy's own questionnaire answers (Oct 2026, sections 1–6). Earlier brief details not in his answers (national-level football/basketball, ankle injury, "nostalgic samples with modern sound", drums, the "music became my new identity" quote, the studio/producer-team vision, the Originality/Community/... values) were dropped from the copy; he plays piano and guitar, not drums. Vision, goals (dream venue Paradiso), audience and his five words (happy, energetic, hard, rhythmic, dancing) now come from sections 4–6.

## Operating Context

- Content changes monthly: demos (autumn 2026), next official release (Jan 2027), first live showcase and 2-hour all-original set (Feb 2027). Track list, events and press assets must be easy to update.
- Brand guide (colours, fonts, logo) is being formalized by the artist; current visuals are provisional.

## Product Principles

1. Booking first: the path from "who is this" to "send an enquiry" is always one tap away.
2. Sound before story: bookers should reach his music within the first screen.
3. Only real proof: show what exists; design honest empty states for what's coming.
4. Built to grow monthly: new tracks, covers, events and press assets drop in without layout changes.
5. Phone-first speed: social traffic lands on mobile.
