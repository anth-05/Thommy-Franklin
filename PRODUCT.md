# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static single-page HTML/CSS/JS (`thommy-franklin.html` with `assets/`). Node/Express backend in `server/` serves the page and an `/api` (booking enquiries by email via SMTP; new features mount as routers). See README.md. Previewed as a Claude artifact link; will be hosted on Thommy's own domain later, where embedded players, a sending form and file downloads become possible.

## Users

Primary: promoters, bookers, labels and press who received a link from Thommy's outreach (Months 5–7 of his artist plan). They check within seconds whether he is worth booking or signing: his sound, story, proof of live play, and how to reach him.

Secondary: fans arriving from TikTok / Instagram Reels / Stories, mostly on phones, who want to listen and follow.

## Product Purpose

The site is Thommy Franklin's portfolio and EPK in one link. It replaces a static PDF press kit, lets bookers hear his music and read his story fast, and turns interest into a booking or press enquiry. Success: enquiries and EPK requests from organizers and labels.

## Positioning

A Dutch UK Garage & House producer and DJ who fuses nostalgic samples with modern sound, backed by real musicianship (guitar, drums, piano) and an origin story: a national-level football and basketball career ended by an ankle injury, after which music became his identity.

## Operating Context

- Traffic arrives from short-form social video, so most visits are on mobile and must load fast.
- Content changes monthly: demos (autumn 2026), next official release (Jan 2027), first live showcase and 2-hour all-original set (Feb 2027). Track list, events and press assets must be easy to update.
- Brand guide (colours, fonts, logo) is being formalized by the artist; current visuals are provisional.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Music: 3 Spotify singles (Don't Care 2026 incl. Funky Swag w/ Say Lez; Keep On Dancing 2025; Energy 2025) and SoundCloud originals and DJ sets, including the Franklin FM mix series.
- Events: none announced; empty state until the first showcase is booked.
- Press kit: short bio, quick facts, EPK on request (downloadable file once hosted).
- Booking: form plus email contact@wildcardmanagement.com.
- Analytics on EPK requests and form submissions is wanted once hosted.
- Open: domain name, press photos, remaining cover art, who updates the site monthly.

## Product Principles

1. Booking first: the path from "who is this" to "send an enquiry" is always one tap away.
2. Sound before story: bookers should reach his music within the first screen.
3. Only real proof: show what exists; design honest empty states for what's coming.
4. Built to grow monthly: new tracks, covers, events and press assets drop in without layout changes.
5. Phone-first speed: social traffic lands on mobile.
