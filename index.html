    Story

    “Sport was taken from me, and music remained. Music became my new identity.”

    “Sport werd me ontnomen, en muziek bleef. Muziek werd mijn nieuwe identiteit.”

    Thommy grew up around music, playing guitar, drums and piano. For years his focus was sport: he played football and basketball at national level, until an ankle injury ended that path.

    Music had always been there in the background. Now it's the main thing. He produces UK Garage & House that mixes nostalgic samples with modern sound, and plays energetic sets that turn that feeling into movement.

    Mission

    Bring people together through energetic UK Garage & House where nostalgia and modern sound meet.

    Vision

    An established producer in the Dutch and European scene, with his own studio and a team of creative producers.
