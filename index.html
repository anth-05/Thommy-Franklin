    Story

    “Every time I hear these genres, joy just comes up. I really can't get enough of it.”

    “Elke keer als ik deze genres hoor, komt er gewoon blijdschap naar boven. Hier kan ik echt geen genoeg van krijgen.”

    I was born and raised in Amsterdam and still live here. I'm an energetic, happy and passionate person who gets along with people easily. There was always a lot of music at home, so in a way it was part of my whole childhood. I played piano and guitar as a kid, but sport stayed number one, until I couldn't play anymore and music took its place.

    As a teenager I always loved making playlists for little things with friends: chilling at each other's place, driving around in the car. I started DJing in August 2022 and producing in November 2023. My highlight so far is my very first club gig at Sissi's Amsterdam: all the passion of the last four years came together, and I got to play an opening set for family and friends with music that genuinely makes me happy.

    I play electronic dance music across a pretty wide range of sub-genres. My sound is rhythm-heavy. Melodic music like Chris Stussy's can be beautiful to listen to, but I'm a much bigger fan of a strong, catchy rhythm that's clearly made for dancing: a catchy vocal that keeps coming back and changes over time, with a small melody now and then for variation. DJing is about reading the dancefloor in front of you, so you never really play just one sub-genre. I go from 127 BPM house to 140 BPM speed garage.

    Inspired by

    Interplanetary Criminal · Overheat · MUCKANIKS · Swingers

    Mission

    An evening or afternoon of two hours of just dancing. For a while you forget you have to go back to work on Monday, or that life isn't going so well, and you dance with people who love the same music. The dancefloor used to be made for dancing, and that vision got a bit lost. Bringing some of it back with music that fits the genre would be a dream.

    Vision

    Short term: giving people music to get them through the day or week, on the bike or in the gym, or to put on at home for their own little dance party. Long term: doing that live, for more people, all over the world.

    Goals

    Making more music I stand behind 100%, playing one of the festivals I always love going to, and hosting my own record label event. Dream venue: Paradiso.

    Who it's for

    I make the music for myself, and sharing it would be nice. For people who just found the festival scene, people who've known what they like for seven years or so, and really anyone who comes to a festival to dance.
